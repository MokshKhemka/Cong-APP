Open the first candidate to see why it ranked, or use Compare top 3 for a side-by-side evidence view. Scores are similarity—not probability.

How are scores calculated?

Scores measure weighted overlap with annotated phenotype profiles. They are not the probability of having a disease. Higher means closer overlap; frequency weights can push scores above 100. Rare findings carry more weight; absent findings and context can reduce a score. This heuristic prototype has not been clinically validated.

03 / How it works

From a messy note to evidence you can inspect.

The app creates a structured starting point for a human reviewer. Every step stays visible, editable, and connected to its source data.

Evidence before conclusions

Built, not wrapped

Real data. Real ranking. No black box.

Notice does not send a prompt to an external model. It parses public clinical datasets locally, builds searchable indexes, and shows the evidence used by its ranking engine.

PythonFlaskVanilla JSHPOHPOA

  2. 02IndexTokens for fast, local phenotype matching
  3. 03RankWeighted overlap, context, and absent findings
  4. 04ExplainMatches, conflicts, gaps, and source links
It does not diagnose a patient.

It organizes clues, surfaces candidate profiles, and makes the ranking logic easier to challenge. A qualified clinician still owns every medical decision.
